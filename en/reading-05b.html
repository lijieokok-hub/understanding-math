Unit 5, Part II: Minimal support and the maximal number of auxiliary positions

This part studies A §§4–5, pp.10–17. At arbitrary finite order, no pointwise theorem for every proper-subset average on general auxiliary systems is available. A first minimizes support among all nonzero correlations, then maximizes the number of auxiliary positions at that fixed support size. Smaller-support correlations must vanish. This determines joint laws of admissible spatial lists, not joint laws of complete states.

Throughout, fix the same nnn, suspension system CCC, selector sequence (Na)(N_a)(Na​), and free ultrafilter U\mathcal UU. Every flow extension uses their pullbacks; they are not chosen anew when comparing different admissible systems.

2. Which parameters change in the results from Unit 4?

2.1 From phase conditioning to distal-parameter conditioning

A Lemma 4.2 (p.11) asserts that (G1-A) and (G2-A) remain valid when conditioning on VVV is replaced by conditioning on the maximal distal factor WYW_YWY​. Further flow extensions retain these laws.

The proof is the same as for B Lemma 5.2 in Unit 4. Clearing the rational speed denominators gives an integer time hhh that fixes every phase. The spatial tuple then evolves under a product of positive integer powers. Ordinary mixing makes this product system mixing. Correlations tend to zero at each fixed phase; a uniform bound and dominated convergence give relative weak mixing over the phase base. This step does not use (HM-A). Higher-order mixing enters the repeated spatial readings in the next step. On extensions, first pull back all existing variables without changing their joint law, then repeat the argument for the new maximal distal factor.
