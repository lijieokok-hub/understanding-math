Moving your progress and sending feedback

The reading pages, search, game, videos and Topic Summary run on this GitHub Pages site. Suggestions and discussions remain on the original site.

Bring your game progress

  1. Open the original game in the browser where you played.
  2. Open the chapter chooser, expand “Keep this journey”, then choose “Export progress”. Save the JSON file.
  3. Open the game on this site. In the same menu, choose “Import progress” and select the file.

Import keeps better chapter records. The file is processed in your browser; this page does not upload or transfer it. Keep a copy of the file. Each browser and site address has its own save, and signing in does not synchronize them.

Reading position and settings

Your old reading positions and theme settings do not move automatically to this address. You can keep the old tab open, follow the corresponding chapter and section here, and resume reading. New positions and preferences are saved locally in this browser.

Send a suggestion or read discussions

Each supported reading page links to its matching original page. A selected quotation can open the original section in the same language; select the passage again there to send your suggestion. Only the public page address and section are in that link, not your selected text or a draft. This static site does not show a successful save or copy private discussions.

Suggestions submitted on the original site retain its private, pending-review process. The site author continues to review and reply there.
