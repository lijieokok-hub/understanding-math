After the successful height: from scalar Fourier expansions to frequency tests

This chapter gives a close reading of the fixed October 5 version of An L3L^3L3 bound for the trilinear Hilbert transform, specifically equation (29) on page 78 and equations (30)–(33) in Section 9.2 on pages 79–81.

The aim is to explain how the finite mode expansion at a successful height becomes a frequency test with decaying weights, and why, with three core roles, the fourth role must carry a squared Fourier mass. Finite sparse counting is treated by Lemma 9.1 in the following Section 9.3, not Section 9.1. That counting lemma is not reproved here.

5. What the four-factor Haar estimate uses

The two adjacent-pair maps both have determinant 111, so this particular L2L^2L2 bound holds on every compact abelian group. 13. Why the absolute error does not count failure histories
