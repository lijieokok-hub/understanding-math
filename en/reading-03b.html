Row estimates and actual projectionsD §§4.3–4.4 · Corollary 4.5, Lemma 4.6

Unit 3, continued: Projection increments and smooth mean-zero rows

This part studies Corollary 4.5 and Lemma 4.6 in D §§4.3–4.4, pp.23–26. The preceding part proved an abstract row estimate for varying functions. We now verify its assumptions for two specific classes of operators.

For projection increments, orthogonality directly proves the joint Bessel bound. For smooth rows, the original Bessel bound remains an explicit hypothesis; the argument shows how to apply it to functions at different depths. Both estimates enter the projection construction and delayed replacement in D §5, and ultimately the local absolute-sum estimate of Theorem 3.2.
