This part studies B §§6.4–6.5, pp.21–24, principally Lemmas 6.4–6.6 and Proposition 6.7. 10. Completion of the paper's fourfold deduction

Conditional on the previously stated results, pointwise failure produces an initial nonzero correlation. The maximal auxiliary-position count exists and survives a stable extension. Regrouping and the square-array calculation then produce one more auxiliary position, ruling out failure.

Finally, the known L2L^2L2 limit identifies the product of the four integrals. Pull back from the standard model for the fixed functions to the original probability space to obtain the conclusion of B Theorem 1.1.
