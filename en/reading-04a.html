Unit 4, Part I: From pointwise failure to a selected law

We turn to §3 of B, beginning with Proposition 3.1 and the first part of Proposition 3.2, pp.7–10. The aim is to encode a possible failure of fourfold pointwise convergence in a countably additive probability measure, retaining a positive correlation, the one-coordinate marginals, and invariance under the action with different coordinate speeds.

Determining the conditional distributions given all parameters also requires distal and relatively weakly mixing structure. That is the subject of Part II. The fourfold main theorem is not treated here as an independently verified conclusion.

1. The two convergence inputs

1.1 Reduction to a standard space

Once four functions are fixed, all their integer translates define a standard Borel factor. See B §2.1, pp.3–4, and the full construction in the standard-factor supplement. Pulling back a Borel full-measure convergence set recovers convergence on the original space, so the final conclusion does not require that space to be standard.

Henceforth let (X,μ,T)(X,\mu,T)(X,μ,T) be a standard Borel probability system, with TTT invertible, bimeasurable, probability-preserving, and mixing. Set I={1,2,3,4}I=\{1,2,3,4\}I={1,2,3,4}.

Scope of this part

Conditional on the pointwise theorem for at most three factors in B Lemma 2.1, and using the L2L^2L2 theorem under ordinary mixing from Unit 1, we have fully derived the positive-correlation selection in Proposition 3.1.

For every standard probability-preserving flow extension, we have also expanded the proof of the existence of the probability measure in Proposition 3.2, its fixed one-coordinate marginals, different-speed invariance, and compatibility with factors and countable inverse limits. The ultrafilter limit was not simply declared countably additive. That property came from representing a positive functional on a compact space and proving concentration of the marginals.

The full distal parameter law in Proposition 3.2 and proper-subset independence conditional on all parameters remain to be proved here. Nor has independence of all four coordinates been asserted: (Retained) preserves a nonzero fourfold correlation.
