Unit 3, extension: finite counting on sparse scales

This part gives a step-by-step proof of H Lemma 9.1, October 5, 2026 version, pp.81–87. It supplies the counting gain at a power below one in D §6.6. Its connection with amplitude interpolation is explained in Sections 5–6 of “Counting core tuples and interpolating amplitudes.”

The proof concerns only the finite proposition under the five stated hypotheses. The source version and its citation in D have been checked against each other. D's application must still supply the frequency gaps, positionwise counts, and scalar kernel estimates.
