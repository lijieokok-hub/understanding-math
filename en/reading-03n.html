Side functions and matched differencesD §6.5 · (6.18)

Unit 3, continued: why two and three non-core positions require different arguments

This part treats the beginning of D §6.5, pp.52–54, especially equation (6.18). Three functions with pathwise square-sum bounds can be summed directly by Hölder's inequality. With exactly two, the projections in the complementary positions must first be removed so that the small correlations of the full YYY become available. A matched difference sum with coefficient P−1P^{-1}P−1 remains.

Far differences in the projection transfer are controlled by the closure and label-separation conclusions of H Lemma 6.9. We prove the finite algebraic identity, identify where the source assumptions enter, and derive the freezing errors from the established variation bounds. The fixed short-block comparison and final P−1/2P^{-1/2}P−1/2 gain are deferred to the next part.

4. Discarding far differences while retaining the whole near sum

The source closure and label-separation results bound the total absolute contribution of all far differences with ∣p−q′∣>2|p-q'|>2∣p−q′∣>2, within each interval and group, by

Dbase−C3∥Sa∥2,I∥Sb∥2,I D_{\mathrm{base}}^{-C_3} \|S_a\|_{2,I}\|S_b\|_{2,I} Dbase−C3​​∥Sa​∥2,I​∥Sb​∥2,I​

where C3C_3C3​ may be prescribed first and the source numerical precision chosen afterward. All main and auxiliary counts must precede the corresponding mismatch tolerances.

The two directions in H Lemma 6.9 use different hypotheses:

  • For p>q′+2p>q'+2p>q′+2, the lower complementary difference consists of source columns from a positive projection. Their labels lie in both sets of use required by the higher auxiliary residual and have lower rank. Apply auxiliary closure with the entire SaS_aSa​ as its basis.
  • For q′>p+2q'>p+2q′>p+2, start from lower-rank source columns in the other complementary position xdx_dxd​. Nearby labels enter the higher complementary residual's set of use through closure under neighboring sets. Distant labels are separated from the auxiliary positive-projection source. Use main closure for the former and the source mismatch estimate for the latter.

The order of filter removal makes the other complementary source strictly lower in rank. A gap greater than two supplies the required one-step adjacency margin. Denominators in the source tests cancel the coefficient bounds for the expanded lower-rank columns. Retained complete functions are not fully expanded in coefficients. These are structural hypotheses of H's transfer result, beyond the algebra of (Transfer).

For the remaining terms, fix

ℓ=q′−p∈{−2,−1,0,1,2}. \ell=q'-p\in\{-2,-1,0,1,2\}. ℓ=q′−p∈{−2,−1,0,1,2}.

Freeze these vectors and the other complementary function at the block start bbb, obtaining CG,b\boldsymbol C_{G,b}CG,b​ and DG,bD_{G,b}DG,b​. This freezing time is bbb, distinct from the mode-expansion time ub=b−4Ku_b=b-4Kub​=b−4K.

In a ball-filter transfer, nonterminal differences are differences of two permitted group endpoints, while the terminal difference is a shared inner function minus a positive group endpoint. 8. The remaining near-difference estimate

The argument has now handled:

  • The ppp-power sums with three or four non-core positions;
  • The transferred main term with exactly two non-core positions;
