Technical appendix: from dyadic blocks of insertion stages to compression at arbitrary spatial depths

This appendix develops the parts of pp. 7–13 of H needed for the basic catalog construction. Chapter 9 only uses a row estimate for insertion stages. Here the rows may lie at different spatial depths, which requires a separate argument. Throughout the proof, all columns, birth cells, and inherited masks are fixed. We do not take a supremum over the adaptive choices themselves.

5. The main term and coefficient error of each row

Assign each row to the deepest attempt root RRR containing its cell. Suppose the nearest selected ancestor of its row cell uuu in this attempt is AAA, at stage j<Tj<Tj<T. The stopping rule implies

6. Summation and scope

H, p. 13 · Compression in the main text · Application to joint Bessel rows

Each row belongs to exactly one size segment and one attempt. In the Hilbert space of all rows, apply the triangle inequality to add (A13)(\mathrm{A13})(A13) and (A14)(\mathrm{A14})(A14). Then use (A6)(\mathrm{A6})(A6), (A7)(\mathrm{A7})(A7), and (A11)(\mathrm{A11})(A11) to obtain

(∑ν∥BνPu(ν)m⋯Pu(ν)1z∥2)1/2≤LTm(2∑K∥gK∥22)1/2+τD3/2(2∑KaK2∣K∣)1/2≤C[LTmlog⁡(2D)+D−17/2]∥z∥2. \begin{aligned} \left(\sum_\nu\|B_\nu P^m_{u(\nu)}\cdots P^1_{u(\nu)}z\|^2\right)^{1/2} &\le L_T^m\left(2\sum_K\|g_K\|_2^2\right)^{1/2} +\tau D^{3/2}\left(2\sum_Ka_K^2|K|\right)^{1/2}\\ &\le C\left[L_T^m\sqrt{\log(2D)}+D^{-17/2}\right]\|z\|_2. \end{aligned} (ν∑​∥Bν​Pu(ν)m​⋯Pu(ν)1​z∥2)1/2​≤LTm​(2K∑​∥gK​∥22​)1/2+τD3/2(2K∑​aK2​∣K∣)1/2≤C[LTm​log(2D)​+D−17/2]∥z∥2​.​

Since LT≤C(m+1)3log⁡(2D)L_T\le C(m+1)^3\log(2D)LT​≤C(m+1)3log(2D), this proves (11)(11)(11) of the main text. For an empty product, use the original row contraction directly. With complementary projections, expand each 1−P1-P1−P; the resulting at most 2m2^m2m terms can still be absorbed into the same bound. For an average with respect to one finite measure fixed for all rows, suppose the projection products are measurable in the parameter and satisfy the stated budget uniformly. The integral triangle inequality in the row space adds only the total variation of the measure. This does not permit a separate measure or projection family for each row.

The argument proves depth-independent spatial compression for any fixed finite column system satisfying the birth-budget and conditioning assumptions. It does not prove the birth budget of an adaptive catalog. Sections 5, 8, and 12 of the main text establish the budgets for old columns, prediction columns, and additional high-slot columns, respectively. Neither result may be used as a premise for the other.
