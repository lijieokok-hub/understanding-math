4. No new independence is needed after selecting offsets

Equation (Average-error) bounds the mean of a nonnegative random quantity. If its right-hand side is positive, Markov's inequality gives offsets for which the total error is at most twice that bound. If the mean is zero, the total error is zero almost everywhere. Fix such offsets and absorb the factor two into the common constant.

The preceding non-core mode energy bound

∑G,b∥Vl,G,b∥22≤G0∣R∣(V-energy) \sum_{G,b}\|V_{l,G,b}\|_2^2\le G_0|R| \tag{V-energy} G,b∑​∥Vl,G,b​∥22​≤G0​∣R∣(V-energy)

is uniform over all candidate offsets and therefore remains valid after this selection.

Later core classification, restriction, stopping, and removal of intervals may depend on the selected offsets. They only remove terms from the already fixed nonnegative error sum and cannot increase it. The required order is to fix the main uses before averaging; later choices need not be independent of the offsets.

After selecting offsets, the full augmented vector Sl,G,sS_{l,G,s}Sl,G,s​ is fixed. D p.50 then invokes the auxiliary-column closure construction from H. Its test objects are these complete vectors and the originally specified ordered projection products; no new auxiliary basis is created for each mode, height, or size. The counts for this construction are already included in the main column constant. The closure result from H is not reproved here.
