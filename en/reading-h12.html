From actual search histories to current-group localization and far-band transfer to side arguments

This chapter explains the filter arrangement, Proposition 6.8, and Lemma 6.9 on pp. 47–52 of H. We use the 93-page version of October 5, 2026. All source page numbers below refer to this version.

The basic columns already constructed describe the components of each argument. Historical masks determine which curvatures may belong to the same group. Finite catalogs ensure that all residual tests in a prescribed finite list can be satisfied. We combine these three facts: first list the projection words actually used by binary search, then prove localization, and finally verify filter transfer in each direction.

One further input remains explicit throughout: the complete side states used as bases for the helper catalog must first be constructed and fixed in Sections 7–8 of H, and their number must satisfy the prescribed bound. We provide all transfer histories needed once these fixed states are supplied. Finite catalog termination alone does not establish that the complete side states already exist.

8. A nonold source matches exactly one current group
