建议服务连接检测
Feedback connection check

此页只检查你的浏览器能否读取公开讨论列表。只有点击按钮才会连接建议服务；不会提交意见、登录或读取私有记录。结果只显示连接状态与条数，不显示讨论正文。

This checks whether your browser can read the public discussion list. It connects only when you press the button. It does not submit suggestions, sign in, or read private records. Results show the connection status and count, never discussion text.

最长等待 15 秒。失败结果不能单独区分网络、浏览器扩展、跨站访问或服务错误。
The check waits up to 15 seconds. A failed request alone cannot distinguish network, browser-extension, cross-origin, or service problems.
